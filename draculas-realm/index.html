What the app does

An audio guide that starts on its own

As you approach a sight, its story begins to play. There is nothing to search for on your phone.

History and myth, kept apart

Every place is marked as history (Vlad the Impaler), fiction (Bram Stoker) or mystery. Bran is never presented as “Dracula's home” without the explanation.

Ready-made routes

From a two-day weekend out of Bucharest to the seven-day circuit, with the stops in order and a badge for every place you visit.

Looking for local partners

The app does not sell tours or take payments: travellers send a free quote request. I am looking for a licensed travel agency to operate the tours, and for guesthouses and guides near the places on the map who would like to be featured in the app.
