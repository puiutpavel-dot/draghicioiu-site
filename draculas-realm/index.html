Dracula's Realm

Tours of Transylvania, in the footsteps of Vlad the Impaler

Private and small-group tours from Bucharest with English-speaking guides, to the castles, citadels and Saxon towns behind the legend. The history of Vlad the Impaler and Bram Stoker's novel are kept apart, so you always know what is fact and what is myth.

Tours and prices

All prices are in euro, per person. Private tour prices depend on the size of your group.

How to book

1. Choose a tour

Pick the tour that fits your time in Romania, from one day to five.

2. Send a request

Tell us your dates and how many people are travelling. A request is free and does not commit you to anything.

3. Get your confirmation

We reply with availability and the final price for your group. No payment is taken on this website.
