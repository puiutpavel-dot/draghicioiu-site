Kinly Circles

iOS app · Daniel Drăghicioiu

Kinly Circles is a social app for small circles — friends, family, your running club. Share photos and 24-hour stories, comment, and message people one-to-one, with reporting and blocking built in.

Frequently asked questions

How do I report a post, comment or account?
Tap ••• on the post, story, profile or conversation and choose Report. For comments, swipe left or long-press. Reports are anonymous and reviewed within 24 hours.

How do I block someone?
Tap ••• on their post, profile or conversation and choose Block. Blocked people can't see your posts or message you. You can unblock them in Settings → Blocked accounts.

Who can see my posts?
When posting, choose Everyone or Followers only. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, posts, photos, stories, comments and messages.
