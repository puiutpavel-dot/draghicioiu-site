Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Kinly is a social app for sharing photos, stories and messages with the people you know. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider.
  • Profile: your name, username, bio, the location text you choose to add, and your profile photo.
  • Content you create: posts, photos, stories, comments, likes, saved posts, follows, blocks, reports and direct messages.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or browsing history, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

How we use it

  • To run the service: show your content to the people you choose, deliver messages, and show activity such as likes and new followers.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our Community Guidelines, and remove abusive accounts.
  • To contact you about your account when necessary.

We never sell your personal data and never use it for advertising.

Who can see your content

  • Posts shared with Everyone are visible to signed-in Kinly users. Posts shared with Followers only are visible to your followers.
  • Stories are visible to signed-in users for 24 hours.
  • Direct messages are visible only to the people in the conversation.
  • People you block can't see your profile, posts or stories, and can't message you.
  • Uploaded photos are stored as files that can be opened by anyone who has their direct link.

Where your data is stored

Kinly uses Supabase as its data processor. Data is stored on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union).

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Stories expire after 24 hours. When you delete a post, comment or story, it is removed. When you delete your account, your profile and all of your content are permanently deleted.

Your rights and choices

  • Edit your profile and delete your content at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Delete account.
  • If you are in the EU/EEA you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and you may complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Kinly is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant, we will let you know in the app.
